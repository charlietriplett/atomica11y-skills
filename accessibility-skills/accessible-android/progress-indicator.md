# Android Progress indicator skills



## How to test a progress indicator 

Given that I am on a screen with a progress indicator

### 1. Keyboard only

- WHEN I use the arrow key to browse to a progress indicator

  - I SEE the progress indicator comes into view


### 2. Android screenreader

- WHEN I use a screenreader AND I swipe to browse to a progress indicator

  - I HEAR its purpose is clear and explains what items are waiting

  - I HEAR it identifies itself as a progress bar


### 3. Device settings

- WHEN I use text resize I SEE text content can resize up to 200% without losing information
