# Design Video player skills



## How to test a video player design

A video player must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Captions are available with pre-recorded video with audio content -  1.2.2 A
- Transcript is available for pre-recorded video with audio content - 1.2.8 AAA
- Descriptive transcript is available for pre-recorded video without audio content - 1.2.8 A
- Audio description is available when important information cannot be understood from from the soundtrack alone - 1.2.3 A
- Captions can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Captions have a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### 2. Operable

- Content does not flash more than 3x / second or contains warnings - 2.3.1 A
- Autoplay video is paused by Prefers Reduced Motion device setting - 2.2.2 A
- Autoplay video can be paused, stopped, hidden - 2.2.2 A
- Control click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- Control click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- Control focus indication is visible (in some way) 2.4.7 AA
- Control focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- Control focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### 4. Robust

- Name, role, state of controls is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A video player must work for people with disabilities:

### Photosensitivity

- Content does not flash more than 3x / second or contains warnings - 2.3.1 A

### Vestibular motion sensitivity

- Autoplay video is paused by Prefers Reduced Motion device setting - 2.2.2 A
- Autoplay video can be paused, stopped, hidden - 2.2.2 A

### Deaf or hard of hearing

- Captions are available with pre-recorded video with audio content -  1.2.2 A
- Transcript is available for pre-recorded video with audio content - 1.2.8 AAA

### Low vision

- Captions have a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio - 1.4.3 AA
- Captions can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA

### Motor

- Control click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- Control click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- Control focus indication is visible (in some way) 2.4.7 AA
- Control focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- Control focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### Blindness

- Name, role, state is documented  4.1.2 A
- Descriptive transcript is available for pre-recorded video without audio content - 1.2.8 A
- Audio description is available when important information cannot be understood from from the soundtrack alone - 1.2.3 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
