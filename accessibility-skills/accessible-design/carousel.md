# Design Carousel slideshow skills



## How to test a carousel slideshow design

A carousel slideshow must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA

### 2. Operable

- All functionality is operable with the keyboard - 2.1.1 A
- Controls and content are read/focused in a meaningful sequence - 2.4.3 A
- Includes visible pause, stop and hide controls for automatically moving content - 2.2.2 A
- Instead of swipe gestures, it can also be operated with a single tap - 2.5.1 A
- Motion/animation can be disabled by device reduced motion settings - 2.3.3 AAA

### 4. Robust

- Name, role, state is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A carousel slideshow must work for people with disabilities:

### Cognitive

- Motion/animation can be disabled by device reduced motion settings - 2.3.3 AAA
- Includes visible pause, stop and hide controls for automatically moving content - 2.2.2 A

### Low vision

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA

### Motor

- All functionality is operable with the keyboard - 2.1.1 A
- Instead of swipe gestures, it can also be operated with a single tap - 2.5.1 A

### Blindness

- Name, role, state is documented  4.1.2 A
- Controls and content are read/focused in a meaningful sequence - 2.4.3 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
