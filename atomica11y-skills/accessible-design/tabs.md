# Design Tab group skills



## How to test a tab group design

A tab group must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying state (selected/unselected) - 1.4.1 A
- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA
- The selected state indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio - 1.4.11 A

### 2. Operable

- Keyboard actions are documented - 2.1.1 A
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- The checked, disabled and focus states have a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default 2.4.13 AA
- The focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- The focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### 4. Robust

- Name, role, state is documented - 4.1.2 A
- Expresses its group name/legend 4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A tab group must work for people with disabilities:

### Low vision

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying state (selected/unselected) - 1.4.1 A
- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA
- The selected state indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio - 1.4.11 A

### Color perception

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying state (selected/unselected) - 1.4.1 A

### Motor

- Keyboard actions are documented - 2.1.1 A
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- The selected and focus states have a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default 2.4.13 AA
- The focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- The focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### Blindness

- Name, role, state is documented - 4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
