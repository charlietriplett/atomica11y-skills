# Design Form skills



## How to test a form design

A form must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA
- Color is not used as the only means of conveying meaning (errors, success, required) - 1.4.1 A

### 4. Robust

- Notification of changes are presented to screen reader without receiving focus - 4.1.3 AA
- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A form must work for people with disabilities:

### Low vision

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### Color perception

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying meaning (errors, success, required) - 1.4.1 A

### Blindness

- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
- Notification of changes are available to screen reader
