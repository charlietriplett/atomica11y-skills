# Design Header landmark skills



## How to test a header  design

A header landmark must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### 4. Robust

- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A header landmark must work for people with disabilities:

### Low vision

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### Blindness

- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
