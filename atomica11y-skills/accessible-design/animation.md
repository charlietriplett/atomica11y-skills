# Design Animation &amp; motion skills



## How to test an animation &amp; motion design

An animation &amp; motion must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying meaning - 1.4.1 A
- Controls meet atomic level contrast requirements - 1.4.1 A

### 2. Operable

- No content flashes more than three times per second - 2.3.1 A
- For content automatically starting, lasting more than 5 seconds pause, stop, hide controls are available - 2.2.2 A
- For auto updating information presented in parallel with other content, pause, stop, hide controls are available - 2.2.2 A
- Motion triggered by interaction can be disabled (with reduced motion settings)- 2.3.3 AAA
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- The focus indication is visible (in some way) 2.4.7 AA
- The focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- The focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### 4. Robust

- Name, role, state is documented - 4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

An animation &amp; motion must work for people with disabilities:

### Cognitive

- No content flashes more than three times per second - 2.3.1 A
- For content automatically starting, lasting more than 5 seconds pause, stop, hide controls are available - 2.2.2 A
- For auto updating information presented in parallel with other content, pause, stop, hide controls are available - 2.2.2 A
- Any large sweeping or parallax animation can be disabled (with reduced motion settings)- 2.3.3 AAA
- Motion triggered by interaction can be disabled (with reduced motion settings)- 2.3.3 AAA

### Low vision

- Controls meet atomic level contrast requirements - 1.4.1 A

### Color perception

- Color is not used as the only means of conveying meaning - 1.4.1 A

### Motor

- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 24x24px - 2.5.8 AA
- The click/tap target area includes the label and is no smaller than 44x44px - 2.5.5 AAA
- The focus indication is visible (in some way) 2.4.7 AA
- The focus indication has a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio against default and adjacent elements - 2.4.13 AAA
- The focus indication has a minimum area equal to the width of the element and 2px in height - 2.4.13 AAA

### Blindness

- Name, role, state is documented - 4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
