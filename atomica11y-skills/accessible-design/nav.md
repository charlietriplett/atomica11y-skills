# Design Navigation landmark skills



## How to test a navigation  design

A navigation landmark must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### 2. Operable

- More than one way is available to locate content - 2.4.5 AA

### 3. Understandable

- Navigation items are repeated in the same order - 3.2.3  AA
- Help, support or contact options are presented in the same order - 3.2.6 A

### 4. Robust

- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A navigation landmark must work for people with disabilities:

### Low vision

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### Cognitive

- Help, support or contact options are presented in the same order - 3.2.6 A
- More than one way is available to locate content - 2.4.5 AA

### Blindness

- Name and role is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
