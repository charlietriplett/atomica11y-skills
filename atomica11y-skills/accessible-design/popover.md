# Design Popover skills



## How to test a popover design

A popover must meet WCAG principles:

### 1. Perceivable

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### 2. Operable

- Must not rely solely on mouse hover to activate - 2.1.1 A

### 4. Robust

- Name, role, state is documented  4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA

A popover must work for people with disabilities:

### Low vision

- Text can resize up to 200% with no loss of information - 1.4.4 AA
- Text has a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio 1.4.3 AA

### Motor

- Must not rely solely on mouse hover to activate - 2.1.1 A

### Blindness

- Name and role is documented - 4.1.2 A
- Meets criteria across platforms, devices and viewports 4.0.0 AA
