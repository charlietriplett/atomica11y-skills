# iOS Carousel slideshow skills



## How to test a carousel slideshow 

Given that I am on a screen with a carousel slideshow

### 1. Keyboard only

- WHEN I use the ctrl + tab key or arrow key to move focus to a slide or control

  - I SEE focus is visually indicated (strongly if high contrast enabled)

- THEN when I use the spacebar to activate the slide or control
 
  - I SEE the action occurs


### 2. VoiceOver screenreader

- WHEN I use a screenreader AND I swipe to focus on a slide or control

  - I HEAR the purpose of each item is clear and matches visible content

  - I HEAR it identifies itself as &quot;adjustable&quot; plus any custom gestures (ex: swipe up/down with one finger)

  - I HEAR its index is announced (ex: 3 of 5)

- THEN when I doubletap with the slide or control in focus
 
  - I HEAR the action occurs

- OR when I doubletap with the single point tap control in focus
 
  - I HEAR the action occurs


### 3. Device settings

- WHEN I use text resize I SEE text can resize up to 200% without losing information

- WHEN I use increased contrast I SEE focus outline becomes high contrast
