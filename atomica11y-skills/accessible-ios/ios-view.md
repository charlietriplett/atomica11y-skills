# iOS iOS app view skills



## How to test an iOS app view 

Given that I am on a screen with an ios app view

### 1. Keyboard only

- WHEN I use the keyboard to open a new iOS view

  - I SEE the view has a unique meaningful title heading


### 2. VoiceOver screenreader

- WHEN I use a screenreader AND I swipe to browse the view

  - I HEAR the page has a unique meaningful title heading

- THEN when I change orientations
 
  - I HEAR content is accessible in landscape or portrait orientation


### 3. Device settings

- WHEN I use text resize I SEE text can resize up to 200% without losing information
